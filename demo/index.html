01 / TRY IT

Break the symmetry.

Clone A into B, then flip one bit. The coloured wedge is the footprint of that tiny intervention. Play advances the top row; the rows below look into its future.

02 / THE TRICK

No history to remember.

The chip keeps just the present and past of each world. Its rule reconstructs the next—or previous—state exactly. The VGA scan borrows those states, draws 60 rows, then rewinds itself.

03 / THE CATCH

A new past, too.

Changing a bit changes the entire trajectory. Rewind reverses evolution, not your interventions. To undo a flip, flip the same cell again at the same generation.

Interactive reference model · RTL and physical verification live in the repository.
Reversible simulation in ordinary CMOS. No CPU. No framebuffer. No claim of zero-energy computing.
